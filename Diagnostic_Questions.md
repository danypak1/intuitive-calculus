# Pre-Course Diagnostic: Algebra Skills Check

## Before you begin

This calculus course assumes you're comfortable with a handful of algebra skills — not because you need to be an expert, but because Unit 1 (and the units right after it) will lean on these constantly, especially **plugging expressions into functions** and **expanding things like $(a+h)^2$**.

**This is not a test to pass or fail.** It's a map. Work through it without a calculator, check your answers against the key, and use the "What to review" guide at the end to figure out where to spend 15–20 minutes before you start Unit 1. If a topic feels shaky, that's completely normal — that's exactly what this diagnostic is for.

Take your time. There's no clock here.

---

## Section 1: Order of Operations

1. Evaluate: $3 + 4\times2^2$
2. Evaluate: $(5-2)\times(7-4)$
3. Evaluate: $12 \div 3 \times 2$

## Section 2: Working with Fractions

4. Compute: $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}$
5. Compute: $\dfrac{2}{5}\times\dfrac{3}{4}$
6. Compute: $\dfrac{3}{4}\div\dfrac{1}{2}$
7. Simplify: $\dfrac{6}{8}$

## Section 3: Negative Numbers

8. Compute: $-5+8$
9. Compute: $-3\times(-4)$
10. Compute: $7-(-2)$

## Section 4: Exponents

11. Evaluate: $2^3$
12. Evaluate: $(-3)^2$
13. Simplify (write as a single power): $x^2\cdot x^3$
14. Evaluate: $5^0$

## Section 5: Expanding and Distributing

15. Expand: $3(x+4)$
16. Expand: $(x+2)^2$
17. Expand, in terms of $x$ and $h$: $(x+h)^2$
18. Expand: $(a+3)(a-3)$

## Section 6: Factoring

19. Factor: $x^2+5x+6$
20. Factor: $x^2-9$
21. Factor out the common factor: $4x+8$

## Section 7: Function Notation and Evaluation

22. If $f(x)=x^2+1$, find $f(3)$.
23. If $f(x)=2x-5$, find $f(-2)$.
24. If $f(x)=x^2+3x$, find $f(a+h)$. (Just substitute and expand — don't simplify further.)
25. If $f(x)=x^2$, find $f(a+h)-f(a)$.

## Section 8: Simplifying Algebraic Fractions

26. Simplify: $\dfrac{2xh+h^2}{h}$ (factor the top, then cancel)
27. Combine into a single fraction: $\dfrac1x-\dfrac12$

## Section 9: Radicals (Square Roots)

28. Simplify: $\sqrt{16}$
29. Simplify (as a simplified radical): $\sqrt{50}$
30. Rationalize the denominator: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$
31. Evaluate: $\sqrt9+\sqrt4$

## Section 10: Solving Linear Equations

32. Solve for $x$: $2x+5=13$
33. Solve for $x$: $3(x-1)=12$

## Section 11: Slope and Lines

34. Find the slope of the line through $(1,2)$ and $(4,11)$.
35. Write the equation of a line with slope $2$ that passes through $(0,5)$.

## Section 12: The Coordinate Plane

36. Which quadrant is the point $(-3,5)$ in?
