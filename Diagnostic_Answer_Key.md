# Pre-Course Diagnostic: Answer Key

## How to use this key

Check your answers below. For each section, if you missed **one** question, just glance at the fix. If you missed **most or all** of a section, that's your signal to spend a little focused time there before Unit 1 — use the "What to review" guide at the very end to know exactly where to look.

## Section 1: Order of Operations

**1.** $3+4\times2^2 = 3+4\times4 = 3+16 = 19$

**2.** $(5-2)\times(7-4) = 3\times3 = 9$

**3.** $12\div3\times2 = 4\times2 = 8$ (left to right, since division and multiplication have equal priority)

## Section 8: Simplifying Algebraic Fractions

## What to review, by section

Not all of these carry equal weight. Some skills are things the course genuinely assumes you already have; others get re-taught early on, so a shaky score there isn't a big red flag.

**High priority — brush up before Unit 1 if you missed 2+ in these sections.** These are assumed background and won't be retaught:

| Section | Topic | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order of operations | Everywhere, constantly |
| 2 | Fractions | Everywhere, constantly |
| 3 | Negative numbers | Everywhere, constantly |
| 6 | Factoring | Units 2, 6, 9, and most of Chapter 4 |
| 10 | Solving linear equations | Everywhere |
| 11 | Slope and lines | Unit 1 itself, and tangent lines throughout the course |

**Lower priority — don't stress if these felt rusty.** The course actually re-teaches these skills, step by step, right at the start:

| Section | Topic | Where it's taught (or re-taught) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Exponents | Reviewed naturally in Units 1, 3, and 9 |
| 5 | Expanding binomials like $(a+h)^2$ | This is *the* core skill of Unit 1 itself — you'll get heavy practice there |
| 7 | Function notation, $f(a+h)$ | Also central to Unit 1 — expect a lot of repetition |
| 8 | Simplifying algebraic fractions | Practiced directly in Units 1 and 2 |
| 9 | Radicals and rationalizing | Explicitly taught as a technique in Units 1 and 2 (the "conjugate trick") |
| 12 | Coordinate plane basics | Used lightly throughout; picks up naturally |

**Bottom line:** if your weak spots are concentrated in Sections 1, 2, 3, 6, 10, or 11, take a short detour before starting — a quick review video or practice set on fractions, factoring, or solving equations will pay off immediately. If your weak spots are in Sections 4, 5, 7, 8, or 9, don't worry — just start Unit 1 as planned, since the course builds those skills back up from scratch anyway.
